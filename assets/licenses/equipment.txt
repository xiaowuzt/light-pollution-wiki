Weapon images and recipe icons

The weapon gallery uses the mod's original geometry, textures and existing Minecraft
development screenshots, imported for this player Wiki at the project owner's request.
Static model renders are labelled separately from in-game screenshots.

Astral Staff and Endless Starsea: geometry emitted by AstralStaffGeometry with the
mod's metal, nebula and star textures. Star-Magnet Bow: original layered item sprites
and their extruded shape. Eternal Starlight (formerly Rift Verdict): the Nether Star Sword model supplied by the
project owner, from Nazgul Fantasy Weaponry VANILLA Vol. 5. Amethyst Riftcleaver and
Amber Sting use the same pack’s Amethyst Axe and Bee Dagger models. Model/texture attribution
remains with its original creators; this Wiki does not relicense those assets.

Minecraft and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks icons retain their original ownership.
No game JARs, Java source or model-source archives are included in the published site.
Source paths and checksums are retained in the local verification manifest only.
